3. PORTSCAN FALSE-POSITIVE INVESTIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The PortScan Isolation Forest detector was trained on CIC-IDS2017 Tuesday normal baseline
flows with a 5% FPR threshold of +0.026465 on negative decision_function scores.
- Observation: High-throughput TLS/HTTPS flows (e.g. 3067 packets, 1198 packets, 887 packets)
  produced Isolation Forest scores between +0.0613 and +0.1924, exceeding the threshold.
- Reason: In CIC-IDS2017 baseline, normal enterprise flows had small average forward segment
  sizes and low ACK/PSH counts. Modern Wi-Fi TLS streams are statistical outliers relative
  to that baseline.
- Finding: Single-flow Isolation Forest is only a weak statistical anomaly indicator in live
  streaming traffic. Without multi-destination host fan-out corroboration, single flow
  isolation scores cannot be interpreted as confirmed reconnaissance or port scanning.

5. WARM-UP & CAUSAL STATE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A live causal detector cannot make reliable periodicity claims when host observation history
is near zero.
- Defect Identified: When FeatureAdapter initialized with empty rolling deques, initial
  flows evaluated with len(prev_iats) < 2 defaulted iat_cv to 0.0, causing iat_regularity
  to compute as 1.0 / (1.0 + 0.0) = 1.0 (perfect beaconing).
- Adopted Warm-Up Policy:
  * Minimum source flow history: Src_Flows_BeforeOrAtT >= 3
  * Minimum source-destination flow history: SrcDst_Flows_BeforeOrAtT >= 3 (at least 2 IAT intervals)
  * If conditions are not satisfied, detector output is marked as INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY,
    IAT_Regularity_Causal is defaulted to 0.0, and aggressive threat classification is suppressed.


6. ALERT SEVERITY ESCALATION & CASCADING AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Defect Identified: Step 16 AlertEngine automatically assigned severity = "CRITICAL",
  threat_class = "MULTI_THREAT", and forced confidence = 1.0 whenever more than one detector
  exceeded basic baseline thresholds (e.g. C2 score >= 0.50 and PortScan score >= 0.0265).
- Triage Fix:
  * Detector output is decoupled from alert severity triage.
  * In UNLABELED_LIVE context, multiple weak anomalies do NOT escalate to CRITICAL.
  * MULTI_THREAT requires at least two MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE detections or one HIGH_CONFIDENCE
    corroboration.
  * Introduced explicit confidence bands: LOW_CONFIDENCE, MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE, HIGH_CONFIDENCE.
